## Project background

City Harbor used to run its leasing pipeline on spreadsheets and WeChat groups. Accounts were scattered, contract expiry dates were tracked by hand, and there was no easy way to see which units were still available. The goal was to consolidate all of this into a single back-office system.

## What I built

- **Accounts and permissions.** Separate roles for park staff and management, so each group only sees the pages and data it needs.
- **Leads and tasks.** One flow from lead capture through to signed contract.
- **Contract management.** A ledger with automatic calculation and expiry reminders.
- **Floor plans.** A visual view of the units still available.

## Outcome

After launch, contract expiry reminders moved from manual checking to automatic
notifications, and the status of every lead became visible at any time.
