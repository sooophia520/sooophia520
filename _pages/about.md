I'm Fu Yaojia (傅姚葭), a student of Information Management and Information Systems at Zhejiang University of Technology. The subject sits between people and the systems built for them, which is the part I find worth the trouble: what a system should keep, who should be allowed to see it, and when it is simple enough to be used rather than admired. I like the moment something stops being messy.

Outside of class I sing in a choir — several performances at the Hangzhou Grand Theatre, the cultural performance for the G20 Hangzhou Summit, and a choral festival in France.
